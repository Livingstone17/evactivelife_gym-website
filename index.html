Questions

Before you come in.

Do I need experience to join?

None at all. Most of our members start as complete beginners, and your first sessions are spent learning the movements with a coach beside you.

What does the free trial include?

Two full weeks of floor access plus one coached session. No card, no commitment — if it isn’t for you, you simply stop coming.

What should I bring?

Training clothes, a water bottle and a towel. We provide chalk, plates and everything on the floor.

Is there parking?

Yes — space in front of the building on Fatade Road, plus the Ayedun bus stop is a short walk away.

Can I freeze my membership?

You can pause once per year for up to four weeks, and cancel any time before your next billing date.
